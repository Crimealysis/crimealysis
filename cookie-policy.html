Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026. This policy explains how crimealysis.com uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites function properly and to provide information to site owners.

2. Cookies we use

crimealysis.com does not currently use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. The site does not set any non-essential cookies. If this changes in future (for example, if we add analytics), we will update this policy and, where required, request your consent via a cookie banner.

3. Third-party services

Our contact form is processed by Formspree, a third-party service. Formspree may set cookies or use similar technologies as part of processing form submissions. Please refer to Formspree's Privacy Policy for details.

4. Managing cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting cookies. Note that restricting cookies may affect the functionality of some websites.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the technologies we use. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
