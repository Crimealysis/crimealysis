Our approach to remote work

How we work as a distributed team, and what that means day to day.

🏠

No office requirement

Every role at Crimealysis is remote. We don't maintain a central office, and there's no expectation to relocate or work from a specific location.

💬

Async-first communication

We default to written, asynchronous updates so people can do focused work on their own schedule, with regular syncs to keep the team aligned.

🎯

Hired for output

We evaluate people on the quality of their work, not the hours they're logged in or where they're sitting.
